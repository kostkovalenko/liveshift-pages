Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 21, 2026

LiveShift (“the app”) is designed to help users track work shifts, working time, breaks, earnings, and related notifications. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, how that information is used, and what control you have over it.

Information We Access

  • Calendar data — only if you grant access, to detect work shifts from the calendar you select.
  • Notifications permission — to send local notifications and Live Activity updates related to shifts and breaks.
  • App settings and shift history — stored locally on your device so the app can function properly.
  • Device information — limited device and system version details may be included in feedback emails or server logs for diagnostics.

How We Use Information

  • To show your current shift state, worked time, breaks, and earnings.
  • To detect upcoming shifts from your selected work calendar.
  • To deliver local notifications and Live Activities.
  • To support premium features such as faster Live Activity updates.
  • To help diagnose technical issues if you contact support or send feedback.

Data Storage

Shift history, settings, and active session data are primarily stored on your device. If server-backed Live Activity updates are enabled, limited shift-related metadata may be sent to the LiveShift server so remote updates can be delivered.

Server Data

When server-backed Live Activity updates are active, the service may process limited data such as:

  • shift title, start time, wage, and current state,
  • whether lunch is active,
  • device model and system version,
  • Live Activity identifiers and push-related tokens.

This information is used only to operate Live Activity delivery and related diagnostics.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is not shared with third parties except as needed to operate Apple platform features, such as notifications, in-app purchases, and Live Activities.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke calendar access in iOS Settings.
  • You can deny or revoke notification access in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete the app to remove locally stored app data from the device.

Children’s Privacy

LiveShift is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact support through the support page linked from the App Store listing.
